HOW THE WEEKEND RUNS

Three moves, one weekend

No pre-built engines, no head start. Everybody opens the same blank screen at the same minute and figures it out from there.

  1. 01

    Theme drops

    Friday at 6pm the theme goes live. You find out what you are making at the exact moment everyone else does.

  2. 02

    Build all weekend

    48 hours to make something playable. Sleep is optional, snacks are not, and the help pit stays open the whole time.

  3. 03

    Ship and play

    Sunday at 6pm hands come off the keyboard. Every team demos, everyone plays everyone, and the room picks its favorites.
