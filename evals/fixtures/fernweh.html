Fernweh Trips Why we walk Book

Eight days on foot, nothing booked but the path.

Fernweh runs small walking trips for eight people at a time. No coach transfers, no packed itinerary — just a route, a host who lives there, and the unhurried distance a person can cover between breakfast and dusk. We carry your bags ahead so you carry only the day.

The Carmarthen ridgeline

Walk 02

The Alentejo coast

Cork oak to cliff-edge along Portugal's quiet south. Warm light, flat miles, fish grilled where it lands.
