## 12 · Letter

First-person, written, intimate. Opens with a greeting. Reads as a personal note from the founder.

- **Heading:** the salutation in the display italic ("Dear reader,"), 1.5 to 2 times body size; the whole line one style.
- **Body:** prose, single column, narrow measure (50ch), as if typed.
- **Divider:** paragraph spacing only; an occasional `* * *` separator.
- **Button:** no button. The sign-off link at the foot carries a verb ("Join the list"). In the fold the one visible action is the link in the first paragraph (44b).
- **Image:** one signature scan, or a quiet inline photograph, or nothing.
- **Reveal:** none.

Reach for it for personal brands, indie founder announcements, sabbatical or pivot pages, donation appeals.

Avoid for transactional commerce. Letter is intimate; commerce is functional.

Compositions: the Corridor hero (`layout.md` § 6); the footer is a letter close (§ 7).

**Sample opening lines** (first-person greetings, dated, with a place if it matters):
> *"Hello, I'm Anya."* One line: a name, a full stop.
> *"Saturday, 6:14 a.m. The dough went in at midnight."* Opens on a moment, then explains it (Maple Street Bread).
> *"Hey there. This page is soft because the surface should be soft."* A colloquial open and a principle.
