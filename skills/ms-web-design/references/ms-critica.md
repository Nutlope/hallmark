# ms · Critique passes

The passes live in `vendor/taste-skill/critique-passes.md`: section 1 of taste-skill's critique file, copied verbatim (source and commit in the ORIGEN file at the repo root). Never edit the copy; every adaptation is here.

**How to read them in ms-web-design:**

- The screenshots are the ones the render tier writes (the folds at 1280 x 800, 1440 x 900 and 1920 x 1080, the full page at 1440 and at 375), not taste-skill's own file names. Same order: desktop fold, desktop full page, phone.
- "The direction sheet" is the stamp's theme or idea line and the Picks block's Signature row.
- "The house rules" are the Floor and the Reflex gates in `gates.md`.
- The passes are a way of looking, not new gates: a pass that fails becomes a finding named by the pass ("pass 4, edges: six left edges between the hero and the pricing"), ranked with the others.
- On an app screen, skip the passes about a hero, a fold or a scroll story; keep squint, edges, space and frame, and the cover-the-logo test.
